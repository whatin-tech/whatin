The Placement Roadmap

Your step-by-step master plan to secure a top-tier tech job.

01

Foundation & DSA

Master a programming language (C++, Java, or Python) and build a strong grasp of Data Structures and Algorithms. This is the core of your technical interviews.

02

Core Subjects Mastery

Revise OOPS, Operating Systems (OS), Database Management Systems (DBMS), and Computer Networks (CN). Almost every interview will test these concepts.

03

Development & Projects

Build 2-3 solid projects (Web, App, or AI/ML). Real-world projects show your ability to apply logic and learn modern frameworks (MERN, Firebase, etc.).

04

Aptitude & Soft Skills

Practice Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability for the initial screening rounds. Prepare your introduction and HR answers.
